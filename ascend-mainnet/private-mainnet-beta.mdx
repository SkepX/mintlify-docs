<Warning>
  **Heads up before you scroll:** This is closed mainnet beta. Every parameter below is subject to change. Fees, leverage caps, deposit limits, orderbook state, PNL, balances, all of it can be reset or adjusted as we move through phases. The only thing guaranteed is your initial $25 deposit.
</Warning>

## Markets

World Cup events only at launch. More markets open up as we progress through beta phases. Fees and leverage caps will differ per market as new ones roll out.

## Supported Assets

USDC and USDCx. Nothing else for now.

## Fees

Minimal across the board for closed beta:

| Fee Type | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| **Maker** | 0% |
| **Taker** | 0.05% |
| **Settlement** | 0% |
| **Deposits and withdrawals** | Network gas only, no platform fee |
| **Funding** | No funding payments during closed beta. Event-based contracts settle at resolution. |

Fee structure will change as new markets launch. Expect different fee tiers for different market types (sports, politics, crypto-native events, etc.). Closed beta fees are not indicative of public mainnet fees.

## Leverage

Up to **10x**. Caps vary by market based on liquidity depth and event volatility. New markets will launch with their own leverage limits.

## Deposits and Withdrawals

Near-instant is the goal for public mainnet. Closed beta works differently:

- **$25 deposit cap** per user during this phase
- First deposit triggers **AML/CTF verification**, which takes time on initial pass
- We built our own **non-custodial liquidity bridge** for this
- Target end-to-end: **under 24 hours**

Subsequent flows are faster once you're verified.

## Slippage

Honest answer: slippage might be brutal. Closed beta means thin books, limited participants, and concentrated flow. Expect chaos.

## What Is Guaranteed

- Your initial **$25 deposit** is safe and will be refunded
- Orderbooks may be reset during the phase
- PNL, balances, and other mechanics will scale and may reset as we progress through closed mainnet stages
- Incentives for closed beta participants are being structured and will be communicated separately

## Everything Is Subject to Change

<Note>
  To be explicit: fees, leverage, deposit caps, markets, asset support, orderbook state, and PNL can all be modified, scaled, or reset during closed beta without notice. This phase exists to stress test and surface edges.
</Note>
