Ascend has a simple fee system. **Makers pay 0%. Takers pay 0.040% to start, and pay less as they trade more.**

Your fee level depends only on how much you trade. You do not need to hold any token or fill out any form. Everyone starts at the base level and moves up on their own.

## Fee Tiers

| Tier       |     30D Volume | Taker Fee | Maker Fee |
| ---------- | -------------: | --------: | --------: |
| **Tier 0** |     $0 – $250K |    0.040% |    0.000% |
| **Tier 1** |    $250K – $1M |    0.035% |    0.000% |
| **Tier 2** |      $1M – $5M |    0.032% |    0.000% |
| **Tier 3** |     $5M – $25M |    0.030% |    0.000% |
| **Tier 4** |   $25M – $100M |    0.027% |    0.000% |
| **Tier 5** |      ≥ $100M   |    0.025% |    0.000% |

Makers always pay 0%. Your tier only changes what you pay when you take liquidity from the book.

***

## How Your Tier Is Calculated

<Steps>
  <Step title="Every filled trade counts">
    We add up the size of every trade you fill. Both maker and taker trades count. Orders you place but never fill do not count. Orders you cancel do not count.
  </Step>

  <Step title="We look at the last 30 days">
    Your volume is the last 30 days of trading, not a calendar month. Trades older than 30 days drop off.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Your tier updates once a day">
    Your tier is set once per day at **00:00 UTC** and stays the same all day. If you cross into a new tier in the middle of the day, your fee changes at the next update, not right away.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Everyone starts at Tier 0">
    Every account starts at the base rate. No application. No form. No minimum deposit. Just trade, and your tier goes up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Maker vs Taker

Every trade has two sides. One person was waiting on the book. The other person came in and took their offer. That is what decides which fee you pay.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Maker (0%)">
    You were the one waiting. You put an order on the book, and someone else came and matched it.

    * You placed a **limit order** at a price that did not cross the current spread
    * Your order sat on the book
    * Someone else's order matched yours

    **Example:** The best ask is 64%. You place a limit order to LONG 1,000 USDC at 62%. Your order waits on the bid side. Twenty minutes later, someone sends a SHORT market order and fills you at 62%.

    **Fee: 0.00 USDC, at every tier.**
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Taker (tiered)">
    You were the one taking. Your order matched right away against something already on the book.

    * You placed a **market order**, or
    * You placed a **limit order at a price that crossed the spread**
    * Either way, your order filled right away instead of waiting

    **Example:** The best ask is 64%. You are on Tier 0 and place a market order to LONG 1,000 USDC. It fills at 64%.

    ```
    Fee = 1,000 × 0.0004 = 0.40 USDC
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Whether you are a maker or taker is decided when your order fills, not by the type of order you picked. A limit order that crosses the spread still counts as a taker. If you want to be sure you get the maker rate, place limit orders behind the spread and let the market come to you.
</Note>

## How Fees Are Charged

Fees are based on the size of the trade that filled:

```
Trading Fee = Trade Size × Fee Rate
```

This is the same trade size used for [funding](/market-mechanics/funding-price-alignment). A few things to keep in mind:

* **Fees are based on trade size, not on your margin.** A 500 USDC position at 10x leverage is a 5,000 USDC trade, and the fee is worked out on 5,000.
* **One fill, one fee.** If your order fills across three price levels, that is still one fee, based on the total size.
* **Placing and canceling orders is free.** You only pay when your order actually fills.
* **Opening and closing are two separate trades.** A full round trip means two fees.
* **Your tier is locked in for the day.** The fee you pay is your tier from the last daily update.

## Examples

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Taker fill at Tier 0" icon="1">
    ```
    Trade size = 10,000 USDC
    Taker fee  = 0.040%

    Fee = 10,000 × 0.0004 = 4.00 USDC
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Same fill at Tier 4" icon="2">
    ```
    Trade size = 10,000 USDC
    Taker fee  = 0.027%

    Fee = 10,000 × 0.00027 = 2.70 USDC
    ```

    Same trade, same size, but 32% cheaper. The only reason is your trading volume.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Maker fill" icon="3">
    ```
    Trade size = 10,000 USDC
    Maker fee  = 0.000%

    Fee = 10,000 × 0 = 0.00 USDC
    ```

    Makers pay nothing at every tier, so this number never changes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="One order, both roles" icon="4">
    You place a limit order to LONG 1,000 USDC at 64%. The best ask is 64%, but only 400 USDC is available at that price.

    * **400 USDC fills right away** against the ask → taker
    * **600 USDC waits on the book** at 64% and fills later → maker

    ```
    Taker part = 400 × 0.0004 = 0.16 USDC
    Maker part = 600 × 0      = 0.00 USDC

    Total fee = 0.16 USDC
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Leverage" icon="5">
    Fees are based on trade size, so using leverage makes your fee bigger compared to the margin you put up.

    ```
    Margin    = 500 USDC
    Leverage  = 10x
    Trade size = 5,000 USDC

    Taker fee = 5,000 × 0.0004 = 2.00 USDC   (0.4% of your margin)
    ```
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Round-Trip Cost

Your total cost to open and close depends on how each side fills. Here is a 10,000 USDC position at **Tier 0**:

| Entry | Exit  | Total Fee | Effective Rate |
| ----- | ----- | --------: | -------------: |
| Taker | Taker | 8.00 USDC |         0.080% |
| Taker | Maker | 4.00 USDC |         0.040% |
| Maker | Taker | 4.00 USDC |         0.040% |
| Maker | Maker | 0.00 USDC |         0.000% |

If you are patient and use limit orders on both sides, you pay nothing to trade on Ascend, at any tier.

## Other Costs (Not Trading Fees)

Trading fees are not the only thing that affects your price. These other costs are not fees paid to the protocol:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The spread" icon="arrows-left-right">
    The gap between the best bid and the best ask is the cost of trading right away. If the spread is 1% and you market in and market out, you lose 1% to the book. That is 25 times more than the Tier 0 taker fee.

    Busy markets have small spreads. Quiet markets have big ones. See [CLOB & Matching](/market-mechanics/clob-matching).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Slippage" icon="chart-line">
    Big orders eat through the book. They fill at more than one price, and each price is a bit worse than the last. So your average fill ends up worse than the top price you saw.

    This is about how much liquidity is there, not a fee. Limit orders let you avoid it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Funding" icon="clock">
    Funding is a payment between LONG and SHORT traders. It keeps the Mark Price close to the Index Price. The protocol takes no cut of it.

    Depending on which side you are on, funding is either something you pay or something you earn. See [Funding & Price Alignment](/market-mechanics/funding-price-alignment).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Why Makers Pay Nothing

Prediction markets need deep books to work. A market showing a 63/64 quote is easy to trade. A market showing 55/72 is not, no matter how interesting the topic is.

Charging makers would punish the exact behavior these markets need most. So Ascend does not charge makers, at any tier:

* **Tighter spreads.** Market makers can post prices closer to fair value when it is free to do so.
* **Real depth at every level.** Posting and canceling orders costs nothing.
* **Lower total cost for takers.** The spread you cross is usually bigger than the fee you pay.
* **A level playing field.** A new account can post liquidity on the same terms as the biggest firm.

Takers fund the protocol. Makers build the book.

## Key Points

* Makers pay **0.000%** at every tier.
* Takers start at **0.040%** and pay less as their 30-day volume grows.
* Tiers are based on your last 30 days of filled trades and update once a day at 00:00 UTC.
* You are a maker if your order waited on the book, and a taker if your order took liquidity right away.
* Fees are based on trade size, not on your margin, so leverage makes them bigger.
* Placing and canceling orders is free. You only pay when your order fills.
* Spread, slippage, and funding are separate from trading fees.
* 100% of collected fees go to $ASCEND stakers.
