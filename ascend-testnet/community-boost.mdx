Ascend is bridging communities across the Cardano ecosystem. As part of the **$10K testnet trading competition**, we're introducing **2x score boosters** for holders of select Cardano-native tokens.

If you hold qualifying tokens in your Cardano mainnet wallet while trading on the Ascend testnet, your competition score gets multiplied - letting you climb the leaderboard twice as fast.

The boost applies to one designated market per week, pinned at the top of the Explorer page. This market may rotate each week, so check the Explorer regularly to know where your boost is active.

---

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Cardano wallet">
    Connect any Cardano wallet (e.g. Lace) to Ascend testnet. The same wallet must hold qualifying tokens on Cardano mainnet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hold qualifying tokens at trade time">
    Your wallet is checked for eligible token holdings at the moment you place each trade. No snapshot - you must hold the tokens when the trade is taken.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Boost applied automatically">
    If your wallet qualifies at trade time, your score for that trade is multiplied by **2x** - no extra steps needed. Watch your rank climb.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The 2x boost applies to your **leaderboard score**, not your PnL. It accelerates your ranking based on the same weighted formula (trades, PnL%, active days).
</Note>

## Scoring with Boost

The standard competition formula:

```
Score = 20% x Account PnL% + 70% x No. of Trades + 10% x No. of Active Days
```

With the Community Boost applied:

```
Boosted Score = Score x 2
```

A trader ranked 20th without the boost could effectively compete at the top 10 with consistent activity.

## FAQ

**Which wallet do I need?**
Connect any Cardano wallet to Ascend testnet. The same connected wallet must hold qualifying tokens on Cardano mainnet at the time of each trade.

**How is eligibility checked?**
There is no snapshot. Your mainnet wallet balance is checked live when you place a trade. Hold the tokens when you trade and the boost applies.

**Can I hold multiple qualifying tokens?**
The 2x boost applies once regardless of how many qualifying tokens you hold.
