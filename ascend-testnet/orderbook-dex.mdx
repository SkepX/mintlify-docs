## Overview

Ascend DEX runs on the Midnight Preview network. Before you can trade, you'll need the **1AM wallet** browser extension, Preview **NIGHT** tokens for fees, and **tDUST** for privacy-enabled transactions. This guide walks through the full flow end-to-end.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the 1AM Wallet Extension">
    Add the 1AM wallet to your browser from the Chrome Web Store:

    [Install 1AM Wallet](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/1am/bphnkdkcnfhompoegfpgnkidcjfbojjp?utm_source=ext_app_menu)

    Once installed, complete the initial setup by either creating a new wallet or importing an existing one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch to the Preview Network">
    After your wallet is created, the active network is shown at the top of the 1AM wallet interface, with **Mainnet** selected by default. Open the network dropdown and switch from **Mainnet** to **Preview** to access the Midnight testing environment.

    <Frame caption="1AM wallet: switch the active network to Preview">
      <img src="/images/dex images/changeto preview.png" alt="1AM wallet network selector showing the Preview option" />
    </Frame>

    Wait for the wallet to fully synchronize with the Preview network before continuing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Claim NIGHT from the Test Faucet">
    Once synced, fund the wallet with Preview test assets from the faucet:

    [Midnight Preview Faucet](https://faucet.preview.midnight.network/)

    On the faucet page:

    - Copy your **unshielded receive address** from the 1AM wallet.
    - Paste the address into the faucet request field.
    - Submit the request to receive Preview **NIGHT** tokens.

    The faucet processes the request and sends NIGHT to the connected wallet address. Depending on network conditions, the transfer may take a short period to appear in the wallet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate tDUST">
    After NIGHT is received, generate **tDUST**, which is required for privacy-enabled operations and transactions on Midnight Preview:

    - Open the 1AM wallet dashboard.
    - Locate and select the **DUST** section.
    - Click **Generate Dust** to begin generation.

    <Frame caption="1AM wallet: generate tDUST from your NIGHT balance">
      <img src="/images/dex images/generatedust.png" alt="1AM wallet DUST section with the Generate Dust action" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Your Wallet to Ascend DEX">
    Open Ascend DEX in the same browser where the 1AM wallet extension is installed and unlocked:

    [dex.ascend.market](https://dex.ascend.market/)

    On the homepage, click **Connect Wallet** and select **1AM Wallet**. The extension will open and ask for connection approval. Approve the request to link your wallet to Ascend.

    <Frame caption="Ascend DEX: connect the 1AM wallet">
      <img src="/images/dex images/CONNECT 1AM.png" alt="Ascend DEX Connect Wallet dialog with 1AM Wallet option" />
    </Frame>

    Once connected, your wallet address appears on the platform and Ascend begins syncing balances.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check Your Balance">
    After connecting, Ascend DEX displays available balances from the Preview network. Make sure:

    - your **NIGHT** balance is visible,
    - **tDUST** is available,
    - and the wallet is connected to the **Preview** network.

    <Frame caption="Ascend DEX: verify NIGHT and tDUST balances before swapping">
      <img src="/images/dex images/balance check and swap .png" alt="Ascend DEX showing NIGHT and tDUST balances synced from the Preview network" />
    </Frame>

    If balances do not appear immediately, wait a few moments for the wallet and DEX to finish syncing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build the Transaction and Swap">
    In the swap section, enter the amount you want to trade. When ready, click **Build Transaction**. Ascend prepares the transaction and generates the data required for the swap.

    Click **Swap** once the transaction is built. The 1AM wallet opens with the transaction details for approval. Review the information carefully and approve the transaction.

    <Frame caption="Ascend DEX: order submitted after approval in the 1AM wallet">
      <img src="/images/dex images/order submitted.png" alt="Ascend DEX confirmation showing the swap order submitted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Receive tUSD">
    Once the order is settled, tUSD appears in your wallet.

    <Frame caption="1AM wallet: tUSD received after swap settlement">
      <img src="/images/dex images/tUSD received.png" alt="1AM wallet showing tUSD balance received from the Ascend DEX swap" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  tUSD is not yet registered on Preview, so it currently shows as a hash name in the wallet. The team is working on a USDM integration to display it with its proper ticker.
</Note>
