The **Ascend Spot DEX** is a **verifiable, orderbook-based spot market** for trading between supported digital assets, and a **strong standalone product** in its own right: **Midnight's first orderbook Spot DEX** implementation. It complements Ascend's core **event perpetuals** product by providing **non-expiring, spot** liquidity for users who want to swap, hedge, or rebalance collateral without opening a probability-priced perpetual position.

Built on Midnight's zero-knowledge infrastructure, the Spot DEX brings **privacy, verifiability, and institutional-grade execution** to on-chain spot trading.

## How Spot Trading Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a Spot Pair">
    Select a market listed as `BASE/QUOTE` (e.g. stable vs native asset, or other supported pairs as announced).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place an Order">
    Use **limit orders** (rest on the book at a price and size) or **market orders** (execute against the best available liquidity). Minimum size, tick size, and pair status follow each market's specification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Matching">
    The matching engine pairs compatible bids and offers per CLOB rules. Large orders can **walk the book** and receive a volume-weighted average price across multiple levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settlement">
    On success, balances for the two legs update atomically. Fees are applied per the fee schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Order Types

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Limit Orders">
    You specify a price and size. Your order rests on the book until:

    - It gets matched with an incoming order
    - You cancel it
    - It expires (if you set a time limit)

    **Use when:** You want a specific price and are willing to wait.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Market Orders">
    You specify only size. Your order fills immediately against the best available liquidity on the opposite side.

    Subject to available depth and any max-slippage or notional caps configured in the app.

    **Use when:** You want to enter or exit immediately and accept the current price.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Spot vs Event Perpetuals

| | **Spot DEX** | **Event Perpetuals** |
|---|---|---|
| **Price object** | Asset vs asset (e.g. BASE in QUOTE) | Outcome probability (0-100%) |
| **Position** | Spot balances; no LONG/SHORT | LONG/SHORT on a probability |
| **Leverage** | Unlevered spot | Leverage and margin per market rules |
| **Settlement** | Trade settlement (fills and fees) | Event resolution and deterministic settlement |

<Tip>
Use **spot** to move value between assets. Use **perpetuals** to trade belief about future probabilities with leverage.
</Tip>

## Use Cases

The Ascend Spot DEX leverages Midnight's zero-knowledge architecture to unlock trading scenarios that are impossible on transparent-by-default DEXs.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="MEV-Resistant & Front-Running Proof Trading" icon="shield-halved">
    On most DEXs, pending transactions sit in a public mempool where searchers and validators can see your trade before it executes. They front-run your order, sandwich your swap, and extract value from every transaction.

    Ascend's Spot DEX eliminates this by design. Orders are submitted through Midnight's **shielded execution environment**, where trade details remain private until matched and committed. Validators cannot see order direction, size, or price before execution.

    **Result:** No front-running, no sandwich attacks, no MEV extraction. You get the price you deserve.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Confidential Large Block Trades (Institutional Dark Pools)" icon="vault">
    Institutions moving large positions on transparent DEXs face a fundamental problem: the market sees the order before it fills. A $2M buy order on a public orderbook signals demand and moves the price against the buyer before execution completes.

    The Ascend Spot DEX enables **confidential block trading** where order size and intent remain hidden from other market participants. Large orders execute without broadcasting information to the market, preserving price integrity for the institution.

    **Result:** Institutional-grade block execution on a decentralized venue. No information leakage, no adverse price impact from visibility.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Selective Disclosure for Regulatory Compliance" icon="scale-balanced">
    Regulated entities need to prove compliance (KYC/KYB status, transaction history, jurisdictional eligibility) without exposing their full trading activity to the public chain.

    Midnight's **selective disclosure model** allows traders to prove specific attributes to regulators or counterparties using zero-knowledge proofs without revealing anything else. A fund can prove it is a licensed entity without disclosing its positions. A trader can prove jurisdictional eligibility without revealing their identity on-chain.

    **Result:** Trade on a decentralized venue while meeting institutional and regulatory requirements. Compliance without surveillance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Private OTC-Style Spot Trading for Sensitive Assets" icon="lock">
    Some trades are sensitive by nature: treasury rebalancing, strategic accumulation, early-stage token positions, or cross-chain collateral movements. On transparent chains, every wallet's activity is permanently visible and trivially analyzed.

    The Ascend Spot DEX provides **OTC-grade privacy** on a public orderbook. Trade activity, balances, and counterparty relationships remain confidential. Strategic positions can be built or unwound without broadcasting intent to the entire market.

    **Result:** The execution quality of a professional OTC desk with the trustlessness and settlement guarantees of a decentralized exchange.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
