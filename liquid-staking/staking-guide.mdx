Ascend uses **liquid staking**. Your ASCEND stays in your Cardano wallet the whole time, and **100% of the fees the protocol collects** are paid out to stakers.

Your tokens are never locked in a contract or swapped for a receipt token, you don't delegate them to a validator, and there's no waiting period when you want out. Stake or unstake whenever you like, and the change takes effect as soon as your transaction confirms.

---

## What is Liquid Staking

Traditional staking sends your tokens into a smart contract or to a validator. Once they're in there, they aren't really yours to use. You have to wait through an unbonding period before you can move them again.

Liquid staking removes that constraint. Your tokens stay in your wallet the whole time. You sign a message saying how much ASCEND you're committing, and the protocol records that commitment on-chain. Your wallet balance is your stake. There's no separate pool holding anything.

What that means in practice:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="You keep custody" icon="wallet">
    Tokens never leave your wallet. Your seed phrase still controls them. There is no contract that can be paused, drained, or upgraded out from under you.
  </Card>

  <Card title="No unbonding period" icon="clock">
    There is no 7, 14, or 21 day waiting period. You can unstake at any time, and the change takes effect as soon as your transaction confirms.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Your stake tracks your balance" icon="scale-balanced">
    If you move tokens out of your wallet, your stake automatically adjusts down to whatever you still hold. You cannot earn rewards on tokens you no longer own.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Why Stakers Earn 100% of Protocol Revenue

Every fee Ascend collects goes to stakers. There's no team cut and no buyback layer in between.

Your rewards come from people actually trading on the platform, not from minting new tokens. ASCEND is capped at **22 million** with no further issuance, so what you earn isn't being paid for by diluting your share.

## How Staking Works

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ascend-liquid-staking-umber.vercel.app_stake.png" alt="Ascend Staking Dashboard" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Cardano wallet">
    Open the staking page and connect any Cardano wallet that holds ASCEND. The first time you connect, your wallet address is registered with the protocol so it can track your stake.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign a staking transaction">
    Choose how much ASCEND you want to stake and approve the transaction in your wallet. You're signing a small on-chain transaction that carries metadata describing your action: the asset (ASCEND), the amount, your address, and a one-time identifier so the same approval can't be replayed.

    The transaction is short-lived. It has to reach the chain within 10 minutes of being signed, otherwise it expires and you sign a fresh one. Your tokens never move. It's just there to prove the wallet owner authorized the stake.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The protocol verifies on-chain">
    Once your transaction is in a Cardano block, the protocol reads it directly from the chain to confirm three things:

    - The transaction was signed by the wallet that claims to be staking (verified by checking that one of the inputs comes from your address)
    - The metadata is well-formed and references the ASCEND token specifically
    - The one-time identifier has not been used before

    Only after all of those pass is your stake recorded.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Your stake is active and earning">
    Your wallet is now marked as staked with the amount you committed. Any further stakes from the same wallet get added on top of your existing position instead of replacing it. From here, you start earning your share of fees.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Unstake whenever you want">
    Unstaking is the same flow in reverse. You sign a transaction declaring the amount you want to remove, and once it confirms on-chain your stake is reduced. No waiting period. If you unstake the full amount, the wallet is marked unstaked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Your Balance and Stake Stay in Sync

Because your tokens never leave your wallet, the protocol has to make sure your declared stake matches what you actually hold. It does this with a recurring balance check.

Twice a day, at **00:00 UTC and 12:00 UTC**, the protocol scans every staked wallet and compares your on-chain ASCEND balance against the staked amount on record:

- If your balance is **equal to or greater than** your staked amount, nothing changes.
- If your balance has **dropped below** your staked amount (because you sent some tokens out), your stake is automatically reduced to whatever you currently hold.
- If your balance has gone to **zero**, your wallet is marked fully unstaked.

You keep your tokens the entire time, and rewards are always calculated against what you actually hold.
